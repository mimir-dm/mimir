# Share a Player Link

A player link gives one player access to their own character on their own device. The player does not need the DM token.

## What a Player Gets

The link opens the player page. It has three tabs:

- **Display**: the map that you show, as the player display shows it
- **Character**: the sheet of their character. The player can change it, level up and write notes.
- **Party**: the other PCs of the campaign. A player can read a sheet that its player shares.

A player cannot see your pages, other campaigns or the sheets that are not shared.

## Make a Link

1. Open the campaign and click the **PCs** tab.
2. Click **Player link** in the row of the character.
3. Click **Make a link**.
4. Mimir shows the link and a QR code. They show only now.
5. Let the player scan the QR code. Or click **Copy link** and send the link to the player.

The player opens the link. Their browser keeps the access, and the address changes to `/player`. The player can close the browser and come back to the same address later.

The panel shows "A link is active" with the date it was made. Click **Close** to close the panel.

## Replace a Link

Make a new link when a player loses theirs.

1. Click **Player link** in the row of the character.
2. Click **New link**.
3. Confirm. The old link stops working.
4. Give the new link to the player.

## Revoke a Link

1. Click **Player link** in the row of the character.
2. Click **Revoke**.

The device of the player is signed out at once. It shows "This player link no longer works. Ask the DM for a new one."

## Player Notes

A player writes notes in **Character**, **Notes**. A note written while you show a map of a module is tagged with that module. You can read the notes:

- on the character sheet, in the **Notes** tab
- on the module page, in the **Player notes** tab

You cannot change the notes of a player.

## Party Sharing

A player can share their sheet with the party:

1. The player opens **Character**, then **Details**.
2. The player turns on **Share this sheet with the party (read-only)**.

The other players see **Shared** in their **Party** tab and click **View** to read the sheet. They cannot change it or see its notes. The player turns the switch off to stop sharing.

## Tips

- Treat a link like a password. Anyone with the link can open the character.
- Use a QR code at the table. It is faster than typing.
