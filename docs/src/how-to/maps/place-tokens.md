# Place Tokens

Place player characters, monsters, NPCs, lights, traps and points of interest on a map. You do this on the DM map, before or during play.

## Open the DM Map

1. Open the module page and click the **Maps** tab.
2. Click the map name.

## What You Can Place

The **Place on the map** menu (**Place…**) lists:

| Entry | What it places |
|-------|----------------|
| **Player character** | A PC token |
| **Torch** | A light: 20 ft bright, 40 ft dim |
| **Lantern** | A light: 30 ft bright, 60 ft dim |
| **Candle** | A light: 5 ft bright, 10 ft dim |
| **Trap** | A trap marker |
| **Point of interest** | A marker. A dialog opens for its name, icon and colour. |
| **Monster from the catalog…** | A token of any catalog or homebrew monster. Mimir adds the monster to the module. (Module maps only.) |
| **Monster: …** | A token of a monster of the module (the **Dangers** tab) |
| **NPC: …** | A token of an NPC of the module |

## Place a Token

1. Open the **Place on the map** menu.
2. Choose an entry. The hint "Tap the map to place it." shows, and the **Placement** panel opens at the top left of the map.
3. In the panel, fill in the token:
   - **Name**: required for a PC. For a monster or an NPC, a name here replaces its own.
   - **Colour**: the colour of the token's ring.
   - **Players see it**: turn off to place the token hidden.
4. Click or tap a grid square.
5. For a monster or an NPC, click more squares to place more tokens. Click **Done** in the hint when you finish.

A PC, a light, a trap or a point of interest is placed once. The menu then closes.

## Place a Monster from the Catalog

1. In **Place on the map**, choose **Monster from the catalog…**.
2. In the **Placement** panel, type a name in **Monster name** and click **Search**.
3. Click the monster in the results. The panel shows "Placing: …".
4. Click a grid square. Mimir adds one of that monster to the module and places its token.

## Add All the PCs

Click **Add PCs** in the toolbar. Mimir places a token for each PC of the campaign that has no token on this map.

## Move a Token, a Light or a Marker

1. Drag it to a new square.
2. Release it.

It snaps to the grid. Mimir saves the new place at once.

## Use the Right-Click Menu

Right-click a token, a trap or a point of interest for a menu:

- Token: **Edit** (opens its panel), **Hide** or **Show**, **Remove**.
- Trap: **Hide** or **Show**, **Trigger** or **Reset**, **Remove**.
- Point of interest: **Edit** (opens its dialog), **Hide** or **Show**, **Remove**.

Right-click a light to turn it on or off.

## See All That Is on the Map

1. Click **On the map** in the toolbar. A panel lists the tokens, lights, traps and points of interest.
2. Click a name to select it.
3. Use **Shown** (or **Lit** for a light) to show or hide it, and **×** to remove it.

## Change a Token

Click a token to select it. A panel opens beside the map:

- **Players see it**: turn off to hide the token from the players.
- **Dead**: marks the token dead. The player display shows it grey.
- **Vision preset**: sets the vision fields at once (for example **Darkvision 60 ft**).
- **Vision (feet; empty: no limit)**: **In bright light**, **In dim light**, **Darkvision** and **Carried light**. Click **Save vision**.
- **Remove token**: removes the token from the map, after a confirmation.

Click **Close** to close the panel.

## Tips

- Place monsters before the session.
- Turn off **Players see it** for a surprise.
- Set **Darkvision** to 60 for a PC with darkvision. See [Vision & Lighting](../../reference/vision-and-lighting.md).
