# Place Tokens

Place player characters, monsters, NPCs, lights, traps and points of interest on a map. You do this on the DM map, before or during play.

## Open the DM Map

1. Open the module page and click the **Maps** tab.
2. Click the map name.

## What You Can Place

The **Place on the map** menu (**Place…**) lists:

| Entry | What it places |
|-------|----------------|
| **Player character** | A PC token. The browser asks for its name. |
| **Monster: …** | A token of a monster of the module (the **Dangers** tab) |
| **NPC: …** | A token of an NPC of the module |
| **Torch** | A light: 20 ft bright, 40 ft dim |
| **Lantern** | A light: 30 ft bright, 60 ft dim |
| **Trap** | A trap marker. The browser asks for its name. |
| **Point of interest** | A marker. The browser asks for its name. |

To place a monster, first add it to the module. See [Add Monsters](../modules/add-monsters.md).

## Place a Token

1. Open the **Place on the map** menu.
2. Choose an entry. The hint "Tap the map to place it." shows.
3. Click or tap a grid square.
4. For a monster or an NPC, click more squares to place more tokens. Click **Done** in the hint when you finish.

A PC, a light, a trap or a point of interest is placed once. The menu then closes.

## Move a Token

1. Drag the token to a new square.
2. Release it.

Tokens snap to the grid. Mimir saves the new place at once.

## Change a Token

Click a token to select it. A panel opens beside the map:

- **Players see it**: turn off to hide the token from the players.
- **Dead**: marks the token as dead on your map. This mark is on this page only and is not saved.
- **Vision (feet; empty: no limit)**: **In bright light**, **In dim light**, **Darkvision** and **Carried light**. Click **Save vision**.
- **Remove token**: removes the token from the map, after a confirmation.

Click **Close** to close the panel.

## Tips

- Place monsters before the session.
- Turn off **Players see it** for a surprise.
- Set **Darkvision** to 60 for a PC with darkvision. See [Vision & Lighting](../../reference/vision-and-lighting.md).
