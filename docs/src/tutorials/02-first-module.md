# Your First Module

This tutorial shows you how to make an adventure module. At the end you have a module with a map, monsters and tokens on the map.

**Time:** 15 to 20 minutes

**You will learn how to:**
- Make a module in your campaign
- Add monsters from the catalog
- Upload a map
- Place monster tokens and a light on the map

## Prerequisites

- A campaign and imported catalog data ([Tutorial 1](./01-first-campaign.md))
- A map file: an image (PNG, JPEG, WebP) or a UVTT file (`.dd2vtt`, `.uvtt`). A UVTT file holds the grid, the walls and the doors, so fog of war stops at walls. See [Map Formats](../explanation/map-formats.md). Use a UVTT map if you can.

## What Is a Module?

A **module** is one adventure in your campaign, for example "The Goblin Hideout" or "The Haunted Manor". Each module has its own:

- Documents
- NPCs
- Maps
- Monsters (the **Dangers** tab)
- Player notes

## Step 1: Make a Module

1. Open your campaign.
2. Click the **Modules** tab.
3. Click **New module**.
4. Type a **Name**, for example "Cragmaw Hideout".
5. Choose a **Type**: General, Mystery, Dungeon Crawl, Heist, Horror or Political Intrigue. The type picks the starting documents.
6. Optionally, type a **Description**.
7. Click **Create**.

Mimir opens the module page. It has five tabs: **Documents**, **NPCs**, **Maps**, **Dangers** and **Player notes**.

## Step 2: Add Monsters

1. Click the **Dangers** tab.
2. In **Monster name**, type "goblin".
3. Click **Search**.
4. Click **Add** next to **Goblin**.

The goblin appears in the **Monsters** table. To set how many there are:

1. Click **Edit** in the goblin row.
2. Set **Count** to 3.
3. Optionally, type **DM notes**, for example "On the ridge".
4. Click **Save**.

Click the monster name in the table to see its stat block.

## Step 3: Upload a Map

1. Click the **Maps** tab.
2. Click **Upload a map**.
3. Choose your map file. Mimir fills in **Name** from the file name. Change it if you want.
4. For an image, set **Grid: pixels a square**. A UVTT file has its own grid, so this field does not show.
5. Choose the **Lighting**: Bright, Dim or Dark.
6. Click **Upload**.

The map appears in the **Maps** table.

## Step 4: Open the DM Map

Click the map name in the **Maps** table. The DM map opens. It has a toolbar at the top, the map in the middle and the initiative tracker at the side.

To move around the map:

- Drag the map to pan.
- Use the mouse wheel, or pinch on a tablet, to zoom.
- Click **+**, **−** or **⤢** (fit the map) in the toolbar.

## Step 5: Place the Goblins

1. Open the **Place on the map** menu (**Place…**).
2. Choose **Monster: Goblin**.
3. The hint "Tap the map to place it." shows. Click a grid square on the map.
4. Click more squares to place more goblins.
5. Click **Done** in the hint.

Drag a token to move it. Tokens snap to the grid.

## Step 6: Place a Torch

1. Open the **Place on the map** menu.
2. Choose **Torch**.
3. Click a grid square on the map.

A torch gives bright light for 20 feet and dim light for 20 feet more. Click the torch to select it. The **Lit** switch turns it on or off.

Mimir saves each change at once. There is nothing to save.
